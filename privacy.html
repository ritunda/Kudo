This Privacy Policy explains what information this store ("we", "us") collects when you use our catalog at this website (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. We've kept it short and readable — no legalese.

1What we collect

Information you give us

  • WhatsApp conversation — when you tap "Order on WhatsApp", a message opens on your device with pre-filled details. We only see the message if you send it. Anything you write in that conversation (name, delivery address, phone number, notes) is visible to us.
  • Order details — the items, quantities, variants, and delivery preferences you send us. These are what we use to fulfil your order.
  • Payment details — if you pay via M-Pesa or bank transfer, we see the transaction reference and the name registered to the payment account. We do not receive or store your PIN, full card number, or bank credentials.

Information collected automatically

  • Local device storage — we store small amounts of data in your browser (see section 4) so the site remembers your cart, wishlist, theme, and preferences between visits. This stays on your device.
  • No server-side logs — this site is a static page. We don't run servers, don't log IP addresses, don't track you across other websites.

2How we use it

  • To confirm, fulfil, and deliver your order
  • To reply to your questions on WhatsApp
  • To arrange returns, exchanges, or refunds
  • To send you a "back in stock" message if you asked for one
  • To improve our product range based on what buyers ask for

We do not use your information for marketing unless you specifically ask us to, and we never sell or rent it to anyone.

3What we don't collect

  • No account or password — you never sign up for anything
  • No email address unless you choose to share one in a chat
  • No GPS location or precise device location
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your phone
  • No biometric data
  • No browsing history from other sites
  • No third-party advertising trackers

4Cookies & local storage

We don't use cookies. Instead, we use your browser's localStorage to remember a few things between visits:

  • Your cart contents
  • Your wishlist ("Saved") items
  • Your theme preference (light or dark)
  • Your recently viewed items
  • Your admin PIN (if you're the store owner)

All of this stays on your device. We can't see it, and it never leaves your browser unless you clear it or export it yourself. To clear it, use your browser's "Clear site data" option for this website.

5Third parties

We share only what's necessary with these parties:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Inc.) — the entire ordering flow happens on WhatsApp. Your chat with us is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.
  • M-Pesa (Safaricom) — if you pay via M-Pesa, your transaction is governed by Safaricom's terms.
  • Courier / delivery partners — we share your name, phone number, and delivery address with the person delivering your order. Nothing else.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub. GitHub may collect standard web request logs (IP, user agent) as part of serving any page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Unsplash / image hosts — product images may be loaded from third-party CDNs, which see your IP when the image loads.

6Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Show you what information we hold about you (usually just your WhatsApp conversation)
  • Delete that information from our WhatsApp history
  • Stop messaging you (we'll respect a "do not message" request immediately)

To exercise any of these, just message us on WhatsApp. We'll respond within 7 days.

7Data retention

  • WhatsApp conversations — kept for as long as your order or account relationship is active, and for up to 12 months after your last message, then deleted.
  • Order records — kept for 24 months for returns, warranty, and accounting purposes.
  • Local device data — stays on your device until you clear it. We have no way to see or delete it remotely.

8Children

This Service is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If a parent or guardian believes their child has shared information with us, please contact us and we'll delete it.

9Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe:

  • We never ask for your M-Pesa PIN, bank password, or card details in WhatsApp.
  • Payment confirmation always happens on the same thread you started your order on.
  • We do not share your delivery details with anyone except the courier handling your order.

⚠️ Never send money before we confirm your order on the same WhatsApp thread. If you receive a message from a different number claiming to be us, verify by replying to the original thread first.

10Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced on our WhatsApp status or on the home page of the store.

Questions about your privacy?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll respond within a few hours during business hours.

Refund timing: Refunds are processed within 3 working days of us receiving the returned item, via M-Pesa to the number you paid from.
